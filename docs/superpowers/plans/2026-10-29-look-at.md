# LookAt implementation plan

**Goal:** Persistent eye/head/neck tracking of a target Entity with natural limits and transition back to animation.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-look-at-design.md
**Architecture:** Portable gaze solver and per-character state; isolated mutable model resources; restore before animation and solve after physics/transforms.

Review focus: no cumulative drift for cached/paused poses; independent characters sharing an asset; correct bind-forward axes and world/model conversions; no double-counted parent rotation; clear/target deletion; source model/animation lifetime and system ordering.
